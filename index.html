Common questions

Quick answers before we talk.

How do we get started?

Send me a message through the form or email. I reply within 12 hours, then we have a short call to go over your goal, tools and data. After that you get a clear scope and quote. If we meet on Upwork or Fiverr, the contract and payment stay on that platform.

How much does a project cost and how long does it take?

It depends on how many steps and tools are involved, so I quote after the short call. [EDIT: add a typical range, e.g. “Most small workflows take X–X days.”]

Which tools do you work with?

Mostly Make.com, connected to Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Airtable, Trello, Tally and JotForm, with AI steps from Claude or Gemini where they help. I’m currently learning n8n.

How do you handle my customer data?

I work inside your own accounts with only the access the project needs, and I remove my access at handover. I don’t reuse your data for other projects or to train AI models. If your customers are covered by laws such as GDPR or California privacy rules, we sign a data processing agreement before I touch any data. I build with privacy in mind, but I don’t give legal advice, so you stay responsible for your own legal compliance.

Do you use AI to make decisions about my customers?

AI steps are used only where we agree on them, and I can add an AI disclosure line to chats, texts or emails. Unclear or important cases are sent to a person for review. I don’t build AI screening for hiring, credit, housing or insurance decisions.

What kinds of projects do you decline?

Medical records, children’s data, payment card data and biometric data are outside what I take on. I also don’t build cold SMS or calling from bought or scraped lists, or workflows that skip consent or opt-outs.

What if something breaks after handover?

Every workflow includes error handling and logging, plus a short handover guide so you can see what ran and what failed. I can also monitor and maintain it as part of ongoing AI automation virtual assistant support. [EDIT: add your support window, e.g. “14 days of free fixes after delivery.”]
